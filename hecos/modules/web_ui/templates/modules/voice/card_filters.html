TTS Filters & Sanitization

Filter out unwanted punctuation, LLM errors, or custom words before they are sent to the TTS engines.

Remove Punctuation & Markdown
Helpful for XTTS to avoid pronouncing asterisks or making weird pauses for dots.
Remove LLM Errors
Automatically removes phrases like "As an AI..." or "[Error:]" from being spoken.
XTTS Fix Periods XTTS Only
Converts periods to commas before sending to XTTS2 to prevent the model from saying "punto" aloud. This is a known XTTS v2 bug in Italian and other languages.
Custom Voice Replacements

Automatically swap specific words or symbols before they are pronounced by the TTS engine. For example, replace "..." with a space to avoid weird noises.
